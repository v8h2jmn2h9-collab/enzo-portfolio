Manufacturing Intern

23 Jun – 18 Jul 2025
GRYP 3D · Bordeaux, France

Four weeks inside an automotive additive-manufacturing workshop, following replacement parts from physical references to finished prints. I removed supports, sanded resin, FDM and carbon-reinforced parts, prepared surfaces for electroplating and helped maintain the printers.

I scanned a Ferrari 400i component, modelled a replacement dashboard gear in Fusion 360, and tested and documented a new laser module for the team. Working alongside technicians made me more attentive to fit, finish and repeatable processes.
